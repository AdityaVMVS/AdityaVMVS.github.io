About

I am a Master's student in Computer Engineering at Texas A&M University and before that, I graduated top of my class from SRM University AP with a gold medal in Electronics and Communication Engineering. In between, I spent three years shipping firmware that industrial machines actually depend on.

I like problems that live at the boundary of hardware and software, where a scheduler, a driver, and a signal on an oscilloscope all have to agree with each other. Whether it's an RTOS task, an FPGA pipeline, a kernel scheduler, a computer architecture problem, or a hardware design or verification problem, I am happiest when I am making a real system behave predictably under pressure.
